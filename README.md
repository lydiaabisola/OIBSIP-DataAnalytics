Task 1: EDA on Retail Sales Data
Performed exploratory data analysis on a retail sales dataset (Superstore) to uncover 
sales trends, customer patterns, and product performance.

Key Findings:
- Sales show strong seasonality, peaking every December (holiday season)
- Clear year-over-year growth in Q4 peaks (2015 → 2018)
- Phones and Chairs are the top-performing sub-categories
- Consumer segment drives 52% of all orders

Tools Used:
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

Files:
- EDA Retail sales.ipynb — full analysis notebook
- cleaned_superstore_sales.csv — cleaned dataset
